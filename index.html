Guide & fellow traveler

The White Path

Yaron Etzion teaches The White Path — his own path of meditation, silence, and self-inquiry, inspired by his teacher, Sri Sri Ravi Shankar. Since 1999 he has guided thousands of seekers — through courses, workshops, and one-on-one work with people from all walks of life — into stillness and the direct recognition of the Self.

His teaching bridges the quiet of deep meditation with the ordinary texture of daily work, marriage, parenting, and community — in Edmonton and online.
