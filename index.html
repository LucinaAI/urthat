About Yaron Etzion

A teacher, and always a student.

A spiritual teacher and devoted follower of H.H. Sri Sri Ravi Shankar, Yaron founded the Art of Living's Israel branch in 1999 and has spent more than two decades guiding others into meditation, silence, and the direct knowledge of the Self.

His work bridges the timeless nondual wisdom of Advaita with the ordinary texture of daily life — at home, at work, and in relationship. Today he teaches from Edmonton and online, welcoming seekers from anywhere in the world.
